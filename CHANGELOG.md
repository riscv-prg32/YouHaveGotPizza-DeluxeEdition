# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-30)

First release of the Deluxe Edition: six lessons and an endless second year; the Professor, three kinds of
student, flour, riders and cascades; marks out of 30; attract mode; firmware high scores. Custom indexed
palettes, firmware tiles, dirty-region rendering with a run-time flush probe. Original 6/8 soundtrack on
eight SID-like voices. Validated by the Cartridge Store's ingestion code; not yet run on ESP32-C6 hardware.
